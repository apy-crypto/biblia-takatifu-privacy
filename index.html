Privacy Policy — Biblia Takatifu

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Biblia Takatifu ("the App") is developed and maintained by Happines Gidion Mwakilembe. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Summary

Biblia Takatifu does not collect, store, or share any personal data on any server. Everything you write or save in the App — journal entries, prayers, saved verses, and your settings — stays only on your own device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your name, email, or any account information (the App has no accounts)
  • Your journal entries or prayers
  • Your saved or highlighted verses
  • Your location
  • Any analytics or usage tracking data
  • Any advertising identifiers

Information Stored On Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Verses you save or highlight
  • Journal and prayer entries you write
  • Your reading progress and streaks
  • Your language preference

This information is never transmitted off your device. If you uninstall the App, this information is permanently deleted along with it.

Third-Party Services

Biblia Takatifu does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Biblia Takatifu is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it collects no information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: happygideon02@gmail.com

Kiswahili

Sera ya Faragha — Biblia Takatifu

Ilisasishwa mara ya mwisho: weka tarehe ya leo

Biblia Takatifu ("Programu") imetengenezwa na kutunzwa na jina lako au jina la biashara yako. Sera hii ya Faragha inaeleza jinsi Programu inavyoshughulikia taarifa unapotumia programu hii.

Muhtasari

Biblia Takatifu haikusanyi, kuhifadhi, au kushiriki taarifa binafsi zozote kwenye seva yoyote. Kila kitu unachoandika au kuhifadhi kwenye Programu — maandishi ya shajara, maombi, mistari uliyohifadhi, na mipangilio yako — hubaki kwenye kifaa chako pekee.

Taarifa Ambazo Hatukusanyi

Hatukusanyi, hatutumi, wala hatuna uwezo wa kufikia:

  • Jina lako, barua pepe, au taarifa yoyote ya akaunti (Programu haina akaunti)
  • Maandishi yako ya shajara au maombi
  • Mistari uliyohifadhi au kuweka alama
  • Mahali ulipo
  • Taarifa zozote za ufuatiliaji au uchambuzi wa matumizi
  • Kitambulisho chochote cha matangazo

Taarifa Zinazohifadhiwa Kwenye Kifaa Chako

Programu huhifadhi taarifa zifuatazo kwenye kifaa chako pekee:

  • Mistari uliyohifadhi au kuweka alama
  • Maandishi ya shajara na maombi uliyoandika
  • Maendeleo yako ya kusoma na mfululizo wa siku
  • Mapendeleo yako ya lugha

Taarifa hii haitumwi popote nje ya kifaa chako. Ukiondoa Programu kwenye kifaa chako, taarifa hii hufutwa kabisa pamoja nayo.

Huduma za Wahusika Wengine

Biblia Takatifu haitumii huduma za uchambuzi, matangazo, au ufuatiliaji za wahusika wengine.

Faragha ya Watoto

Biblia Takatifu haikulengwa kwa watoto na haikusanyi taarifa yoyote kutoka kwa mtu yeyote, ikiwemo watoto, kwa kuwa haikusanyi taarifa yoyote kabisa.

Mabadiliko ya Sera Hii

Iwapo sera hii itabadilika baadaye, tutasasisha ukurasa huu na kubadilisha tarehe ya "Ilisasishwa mara ya mwisho" hapo juu.

Wasiliana Nasi

Kwa maswali yoyote kuhusu Sera hii ya Faragha, wasiliana nasi kupitia: weka barua pepe yako hapa
